The problem

Today's AI forgets you.

It forgets you

You explain your work, your preferences and your rules again and again.

It repeats mistakes

Corrections don't stick. Instructions you gave last week get dropped.

It doesn't learn

Every chat starts almost from zero, and you can't see what it knows or why it changed.

The vision

An AI that behaves like a living organism.

  • Remembers every conversation and instruction
  • Thinks across time, not just one chat
  • Learns from what worked and what didn't
  • Grows with you

And humans always stay in control.

How it works

An AI brain on top of any model.

We don't build a new chatbot from scratch. We give the AI models you already know a memory, a rule book and a way to learn.

  1. 01

    Remember

    ACO saves every conversation, fact and standing instruction in a lifelong memory that you can see, edit or delete.

  2. 02

    Think and learn

    Before it answers, ACO looks up what it knows about you and your rules, then asks the AI model you chose. It learns from your thumbs up, thumbs down and corrections.

  3. 03

    Improve, with your OK

    ACO suggests lessons it has learned. Only the ones you approve change how it behaves, and every change is written down.

Why ACO

What makes it different.

Never forgets

A lifelong memory across every conversation. A Memory page shows what ACO knows, linked to where it learned it.

Keeps your rules

Say it once, like “always answer in short points”. ACO keeps it in your Rule Book and checks every answer against it.

Explains itself

When something changes, like a new launch date, ACO keeps both and shows “What changed and why”, with the date.

Works with any model

Use models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. Your memory stays yours when you switch, so you're never locked into one app.
